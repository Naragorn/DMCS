# Step 4c: kneel and execution. With a full meter, the enemy hit last goes down on its knees (alive, brain paused as the game's
# own fear module pauses a cowering NPC, the game's kneeling animation) when no hit lands for a while or only light hits land. Any hit then executes it
# (the critical); unexecuted, it stands up again, or dies with the death chance. Expects the defaults at the start.

wait-level
# Start from the defaults even if an earlier scenario failed before its own reset (saved options carry over)
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
option-exists DMCS "Execution Window" category=Adrenaline type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Kneel And Execution" category=Adrenaline type=Boolean save=true
option-exists DMCS "Kneel After No Hit" category=Adrenaline type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Kneel After Light Hits" category=Adrenaline type=Int32 interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Kneel Death Chance" category=Adrenaline type=Int32 interaction=Slider save=true
expect-option DMCS "Execution Window" 5
expect-option DMCS "Kneel And Execution" true
expect-option DMCS "Kneel After No Hit" 2
expect-option DMCS "Kneel After Light Hits" 2
expect-option DMCS "Kneel Death Chance" 0
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Per Hit" 50
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Window" 20
set-option DMCS "Critical Min Swing Speed" 6

# The NPCs stand 2.5 m away: only an enemy within Kneel Max Distance (3 m, 2026-11-22) kneels; at 3 m the one hit last sat
# on the edge and an older, nearer NPC knelt instead (first run 2026-11-22). The distance rule itself: unit tests.

# 1. No hit for 2 s: the enemy hit last kneels; a hit executes it
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=2.5 angle=60
mark
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
expect-log meter full: window open for 20 s
expect-log kneels (NoHit): execute within 5 s (dynamic animation playing
wait 3
screenshot step4c-kneeling of=npc angle=90
mark
hit-npc Torso velocity=8 type=Slash damage=1
expect-log execution of
expect-log CRITICAL on
expect-log slow-motion Hyperfocus
expect-npc state Dead within=2
expect-log slow-motion over: time back to normal

# 2. Only light hits: kneels; not executed, it stands up again
set-option DMCS "Execution Window" 2
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=2.5 angle=-60
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
mark
hit-npc LeftArm velocity=3
hit-npc LeftArm velocity=3
expect-log kneels (LightHits)
expect-log was not executed and stands up
expect-npc state Alive within=1
expect-no-log CRITICAL on

# 3. Death chance 100 %: not executed, it dies
set-option DMCS "Kneel Death Chance" 100
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=2.5 angle=0 brain=HumanDummy
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
mark
expect-log kneels (NoHit)
expect-log was not executed and dies
expect-npc state Dead within=2

# 4. Executed with a stab: it kneels into death and can be kicked away
set-option DMCS "Kneel Death Chance" 0
set-option DMCS "Execution Window" 5
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=2.5 angle=30 brain=HumanDummy
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
hit-npc Torso velocity=8
mark
expect-log kneels (NoHit)
hit-npc Torso velocity=8 type=Pierce damage=1
expect-log execution of
expect-log chest-stab fatality:
hit-npc Torso by=foot velocity=9 dead=true
expect-log kicked

press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
expect-no-log Exception
screenshot step4c-adrenaline-kneel
quit
