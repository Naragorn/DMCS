# 3. On, holding and coming back each round: won after 2 rounds, its sword flies away. The harness player's sword
# does not touch the frozen enemy blade (player-parry is a raised event, the blades stay apart). Before Clash Hold
# Blade (2026-11-21) nothing held the heavy weapon back: the hand caught up with the moved controller within ~0.4 s
# (measured 2026-11-18) and no lead lasted. Now the hold keeps the weapon at the lock point, so the lead should last
# (not run yet). Difficulty 0 (no enemy push) lets the rounds run either way; how far a real press leads: headset.

mark
await-log 30 blade flashes
player-parry
expect-log blades locked with HumanMale
player-push 0.25 2.3
await-log 3 round 1/2 held
await-log 3 back at the blades
player-push 0.25 2.3
await-log 3 after 2 rounds: its sword flies away

expect-npc holding false within=1
screenshot clash-won of=npc
