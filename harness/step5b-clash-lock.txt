# Step 5b: Clash of Might lock (mode On). A block at the flash point (the game's own block event, raised here with
# player-parry since the harness cannot swing the player's arm) locks the blades: the NPC freezes in its swing, the
# player's weapon gets heavy, a diagnostic is logged every 0.1 s; without pushing, the first round is lost and both are
# released (the rounds themselves: step5c). A block away from the flash stays an ordinary parry. The player's sword in the mock hand also meets real swings now and then:
# those raise the game's own block event too and may lock for real (seen 2026-11-17: contact 0.06 m, 70.9°), so the
# sections check the verdict of their own block, not the absence of any lock. A real block's feel: headset.

wait-level
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
option-exists DMCS "Clash Weapon Weight" category="Clash of Might" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
expect-option DMCS "Clash Weapon Weight" 20
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Charge Stagger Enabled" false
# Every swing flashes, so the next flash comes within a few seconds; the hit of a lost lock keeps the player's sword
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Mode" On
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.2
set-option DMCS "Clash Lose Outcome" Hit
player-invincible true
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 angle=0 container=CivilianMeleeSword
player-grab SwordShortCommon

# 1. Block at the flash: the blades lock, the NPC freezes, the weapon gets heavy; no push, so round 1 is lost after 2 s.
# The harness player's sword hangs as the mock hand holds it, at an angle to the swing that the harness cannot set
# (first run: 64.5°), so the angle tolerance is opened to its maximum (90: any angle) here; section 1b checks the angle refusal.
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 90
mark
await-log 30 blade flashes
player-parry
expect-log blades locked with HumanMale
expect-log x heavier, held at the lock point
expect-log lock t=0.0
expect-log lock t=1.
expect-log the enemy pushed harder in round 1/
expect-log blades unlocked (Decided
expect-log moves again
screenshot clash-lock-after of=npc

# 1b. The same block with a tight angle tolerance: at the flash, but not across the swing, an ordinary parry.
# The block waits for the strike to start: the swing plane (and so the angle) is read in the strike's first frames, and
# a block before that (the blade still at the swing top) has no angle to check and locks (seen 2026-11-20: "angle n/a").
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 10
mark
await-log 30 blade flashes
mark
await-log 5 's swing
player-parry
expect-log : WrongAngle (contact
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 90

# 2. Block away from the flash (0.5 m along the blade): locks too since 2026-11-22 (user: the exact point was hit far
# too rarely; where on the flashing blade the block lands no longer counts)
mark
await-log 30 blade flashes
player-parry offset=0.5
expect-log blades locked with HumanMale
expect-log blades unlocked (Decided

# 3. No flash (min wind-up above every attack): a block changes nothing
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.8
wait 3
mark
player-parry
expect-no-log you blocked
expect-no-log blades locked

# 4. Feature off: nothing
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.2
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Mode" Off
mark
wait 8
player-parry
expect-no-log [DMCS][Clash]
quit
