# Step 5b: Clash of Might lock. A block at the flash point (the game's own block event, raised here with player-parry
# since the harness cannot swing the player's arm) locks the blades: the NPC freezes in its swing, the player's
# weapon gets heavy, a diagnostic is logged every 0.1 s, and after the max duration both are released. A block away
# from the flash stays an ordinary parry. The player's sword in the mock hand also meets real swings now and then:
# those raise the game's own block event too and may lock for real (seen 2026-11-17: contact 0.06 m, 70.9°), so the
# sections check the verdict of their own block, not the absence of any lock. A real block's feel: headset.

wait-level
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
option-exists DMCS "Clash Point Tolerance" category="Clash of Might" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Clash Weapon Weight" category="Clash of Might" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Clash Max Duration" category="Clash of Might" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
expect-option DMCS "Clash Point Tolerance" 0.25
expect-option DMCS "Clash Weapon Weight" 20
expect-option DMCS "Clash Max Duration" 4
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Charge Stagger Enabled" false
# Every swing flashes, so the next flash comes within a few seconds; a short lock keeps the scenario quick
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.2
set-option DMCS "Clash Max Duration" 2
player-invincible true
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 angle=0 container=CivilianMeleeSword
npc-grab SwordShortCommon hand=right
player-grab SwordShortCommon

# 1. Block at the flash: the blades lock, the NPC freezes, the weapon gets heavy, released after 2 s.
# The harness player's sword hangs as the mock hand holds it, at an angle to the swing that the harness cannot set
# (first run: 64.5°), so the angle tolerance is opened to its maximum here; section 1b checks the angle refusal.
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 80
mark
await-log 30 : flash
player-parry
expect-log blades locked with HumanMale
expect-log x heavier for up to 2.00 s
expect-log lock t=0.0
expect-log lock t=1.
expect-log blades unlocked (TimeUp
expect-log moves again
screenshot clash-lock-after of=npc

# 1b. The same block with a tight angle tolerance: at the flash, but not across the swing, an ordinary parry
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 10
mark
await-log 30 : flash
player-parry
expect-log : WrongAngle (contact
set-option DMCS "Clash Block Angle Tolerance" 80

# 2. Block away from the flash (0.5 m along the blade): an ordinary parry, no lock
mark
await-log 30 : flash
player-parry offset=0.5
expect-log : OffPoint (contact
expect-log an ordinary parry

# 3. No flash (min wind-up above every attack): a block changes nothing
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.8
wait 3
mark
player-parry
expect-no-log you blocked
expect-no-log blades locked

# 4. Feature off: nothing
set-option DMCS "Clash Min Wind Up" 0.2
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Enabled" false
mark
wait 8
player-parry
expect-no-log [DMCS][Clash]
quit
