# Step 3c: Twist Disarm. The player's sword sweeps through the standing NPC's sword (real physics: the game's own
# collision events between the two held weapons), the player's right controller rolling around the blade while it does
# (BasHarness player-strike-weapon). Too slow a roll: no disarm; a fast one: the NPC drops its sword. How fast a real
# wrist twist is: headset (the 400°/s default is a guess).

wait-level
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
option-exists DMCS "Twist Disarm" category="Disarm Strike" type=Boolean interaction=ArrowList save=true
option-exists DMCS "Twist Disarm Chance" category="Disarm Strike" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
option-exists DMCS "Twist Min Roll Speed" category="Disarm Strike" type=Single interaction=Slider save=true
expect-option DMCS "Twist Disarm" true
expect-option DMCS "Twist Disarm Chance" 50
expect-option DMCS "Twist Min Roll Speed" 400
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Charge Stagger Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Mode" Off
set-option DMCS "Twist Disarm Chance" 100
# a short no-regrab time, so section 3 can hand the NPC a sword again
set-option DMCS "Disarm No Regrab Time" 2
player-invincible true
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=0.9 angle=0 brain=HumanDummy
npc-grab SwordShortCommon
player-grab SwordShortCommon
wait 1

# 1. The blades meet without a twist: a contact, too slow, the NPC keeps its sword
mark
player-strike-weapon twist=0
expect-log no twist disarm, TooSlow
expect-npc holding true
screenshot twist-slow of=npc

# 2. The same strike with a fast twist (900°/s): the NPC drops its sword. The sweep can knock the player's own sword
# out of the mock hand (seen 2026-11-18), so every strike starts with a fresh one.
wait 1
player-grab SwordShortCommon
mark
player-strike-weapon twist=900
expect-log twist disarm on HumanMale
expect-log (twist, roll
expect-npc holding false within=1
screenshot twist-disarm of=npc

# 3. Twist Disarm off: a fast twist does nothing
wait 3
npc-grab SwordShortCommon
player-grab SwordShortCommon
set-option DMCS "Twist Disarm" false
wait 1
mark
player-strike-weapon twist=900
expect-no-log twist disarm
expect-npc holding true
quit
