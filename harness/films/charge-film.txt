# Film of Charge Stagger: success and failure (not part of the suite). The hit is raised through Creature.Damage
# (no visible swing, push=-1: the game's own hit reaction does not stagger), so what shows is the enemy's reaction.
# 1 standing hit (fails: too slow, nothing happens), 2 sprinting hit (success: it staggers), then a second charge hit
# while staggered (success: it goes to the ground). View: beside the NPC.
wait-level
frame-rate 90
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Mode" Off
player-invincible true
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=4 angle=90 brain=HumanDummy
wait 1

record-start charge-1-standing fps=60 width=640 height=360 views=npc angle=90 distance=3
wait 0.5
mark
hit-npc Torso
expect-log TooSlow
wait 2.5
record-stop

run 1
wait 1.5
expect-player-speed 5
record-start charge-2-sprint fps=60 width=640 height=360 views=npc angle=90 distance=3
wait 0.3
mark
hit-npc RightArm
expect-npc stagger Full within=0
expect-log [DMCS][Charge Stagger] staggered
wait 1.2
mark
hit-npc Torso
stop-run
expect-log [DMCS][Charge Stagger] knocked down
wait 3
record-stop
quit
