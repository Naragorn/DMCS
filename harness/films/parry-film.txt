# Film of Counter Parry and Power Parry (not part of the suite: tools/harness.sh harness/films/parry-film.txt). A
# standing NPC holds a sword; the game's block event is raised twice (player-parry), each time right after the player's
# right controller swept sideways: first at 1.5 m/s (a guard brought up in time: counter, the NPC staggers), then at
# 5 m/s (a hard swing: power parry, its sword flies). Three views: beside the two of them, the player's own eyes, and a
# close-up of the player's hand. Sets no Parry option (defaults), so it runs on an older build too, for the "before" film.
wait-level
frame-rate 90
press DMCS "Reset To Defaults"
set-option DMCS "Debug Logging" true
set-option DMCS "Charge Stagger Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Adrenaline Enabled" false
set-option DMCS "Clash of Might Mode" Off
player-invincible true
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 angle=0 brain=HumanDummy
npc-grab SwordShortCommon
player-grab SwordShortCommon
wait 1
mark
record-start parry fps=60 width=640 height=360 views=side,first,hand
wait 0.3
player-parry swing=1.5
wait 1.2
player-parry swing=5
wait 1.5
record-stop
quit
